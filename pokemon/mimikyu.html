Trainers in the Anime

Jessie

Team Rocket member

One third of Team Rocket, with James and Meowth. In Alola, Mimikyu joined her because it shared her goal of defeating Pikachu. It holds a deep grudge against Pikachu and is strong enough to scare even Team Rocket.

In the video games

Mimikyu debuted in Generation VII. It lurks in the Thrifty Megamart, an abandoned store on Ula'ula Island.
