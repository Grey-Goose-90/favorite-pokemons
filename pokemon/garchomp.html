Trainers in the Anime

Cynthia

Sinnoh Champion

The Champion of the Sinnoh League and a researcher of Pokémon myths. Garchomp is her ace. In Pokémon Journeys she is one of the Masters Eight, the world's strongest Trainers, and her Garchomp Mega Evolves in battle.

Ash Ketchum

Main protagonist, Pokémon the Series (1997–2023)

Ash caught a Gible in Sinnoh. It became famous for chomping on everything, including Ash's head, and for its long struggle to master Draco Meteor. It never evolved during the series.
