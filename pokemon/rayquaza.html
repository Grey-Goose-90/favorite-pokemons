Evolution line Trainers First appearances

Evolution Line

Dragon Ascent

Rayquaza doesn't evolve, but it can Mega Evolve by knowing the move Dragon Ascent. It needs no Mega Stone.

Trainers in the Anime

Roy

Protagonist, Pokémon Horizons

One of the two lead characters of Pokémon Horizons. Roy carries the Ancient Poké Ball, which holds the Black Rayquaza. That Rayquaza was once the partner of the legendary explorer Lucius, and it later answers to Roy.

First Appearances

In the animated series

In the video games

Rayquaza debuted in Generation III. It sleeps at the top of Sky Pillar, and it is the cover Legendary of Emerald.
