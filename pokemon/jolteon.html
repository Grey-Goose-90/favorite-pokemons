Jolteon

Electric
Evolution line Trainers First appearances

Evolution Line

Thunder Stone

Eevee can evolve into eight different Pokémon. Jolteon is one of the three originals.

Trainers in the Anime

Sparky

One of the Eevee Brothers

Sparky and his brothers Pyro and Rainer each evolved their Eevee, and they pressured their youngest brother Mikey to do the same. Sparky's Jolteon battled Ash in “The Battling Eevee Brothers”.

Virgil

Eevee specialist, Unova

A member of the Pokémon Rescue Squad whose team is made of Eevee evolutions. Virgil won the Unova League (Vertress Conference), and Jolteon was part of his winning team.

First Appearances

In the animated series

In the video games

Jolteon debuted in Generation I. Using a Thunder Stone on Eevee, from Celadon City, evolves it.
