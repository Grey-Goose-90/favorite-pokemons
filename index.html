#0445

Garchomp

DragonGround
Evolutions
Gible → Gabite → Garchomp → Mega Garchomp
Anime trainers
Cynthia (her signature ace, Sinnoh Champion) · Ash raised a Gible in Diamond & Pearl
First appearance
Games: Pokémon Diamond & Pearl (2006) · Anime: Diamond & Pearl series, as Cynthia's partner
Fun facts
  • A pseudo-legendary, with a base stat total of 600.
  • Its design mixes a hammerhead shark and a jet plane, and the Pokédex says it flies as fast as one.
  • Hidden Ability Rough Skin damages attackers that touch it.

Strong against

Dragon, Fire, Electric, Poison, Rock, Steel

Weak against

Ice ×4, Dragon, Fairy

Immune to Electric · Resists Fire, Poison, Rock

#0384 · Legendary

Rayquaza

DragonFlying
Evolutions
Rayquaza (no evolutions) → Mega Rayquaza
Anime trainers
None — a wild Legendary. Ash and friends encounter it, but no main-series trainer owns one.
First appearance
Games: Pokémon Ruby & Sapphire (2002) · Anime: Pokémon: Destiny Deoxys (2004 movie)
Fun facts
  • Lives in the ozone layer and feeds on water and particles in the atmosphere.
  • Settles the fight between Groudon and Kyogre.
  • Mega-evolves by learning Dragon Ascent — no Mega Stone needed.

Strong against

Dragon, Grass, Fighting, Bug

Weak against

Ice ×4, Rock, Dragon, Fairy

Immune to Ground · Resists Grass ×¼, Fire, Water, Fighting, Bug

WaterDark
